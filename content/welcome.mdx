# Introduction Terminal

## Welcome to my portfolio terminal Mr. Visitor.

You've just stumbled into the corner of the internet where I keep my
projects, my resume, and the occasional bad joke. Feel free to poke
around — nothing here bites, except maybe the printer question.

A few things you can do from here:

- Type `about` in the terminal to learn who I am
- Type `projects` to see what I've been building
- Type `resume` to unlock my full resume panel
- Type `contact` if you'd like to reach out

## A bit about this site

This portfolio is built with Next.js, styled with Tailwind CSS, and
this very page is written in MDX — Markdown with a bit of React mixed
in. It's rendered straight into the resume panel and doubles as a
sample blog post at `/blog/welcome`.

Thanks for stopping by, and enjoy exploring.
